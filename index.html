Cochlear implants are not just expensive — they require invasive neurosurgery and eligibility criteria that exclude many. For the majority of the world's deaf population, the barrier isn't medical. It's financial.

Cochlea Cap approaches the problem differently. Rather than restoring hearing, it extends what deaf individuals already excel at — forward-focused awareness and lip-reading — to all four directions, through precise haptic signals on the wrist.

How It Works

From sound to safety.
In under a second.

01
Step 01 / 04

Sound detected

Microphones built into the Cochlea Cap continuously monitor ambient audio. When a sound exceeds the 95 dB alert threshold — the level of an approaching vehicle — the system activates instantly.

Step 02 / 04

Direction calculated

An onboard microcontroller analyzes the time-of-arrival difference between microphones to determine precisely which of four directions — front, back, left, or right — the sound originated from.

Step 03 / 04

Haptic alert fires

The corresponding vibration motor on the armband pulses — a clear, unmistakable signal. Four motors, one for each direction, mounted precisely so the user immediately knows the source without looking.

Step 04 / 04

User moves safely

Deaf individuals are already expert forward observers and lip-readers. Cochlea Cap extends that same awareness behind them and to both sides — complete 360° coverage. The user reacts, and stays safe.

Feasibility

Tested in Dhaka —
one of the noisiest
cities on earth.

Background Noise

Street-level measurements recorded ambient noise ranging from 50 dB to 85 dB — a baseline of constant, intense urban sound.

Speed & Intensity Correlation

Vehicles traveling at higher speeds produce significantly louder horns. Trucks consistently outpaced local buses in both speed and horn intensity.

Smart Threshold — 95 dB

By setting the alert threshold to 95 dB, the device activates only for loud, approaching vehicles — filtering out the constant sub-85 dB ambient crowd noise with no false positives.

What's Next

The road ahead.

Three forward milestones, each expanding the reach and intelligence of the platform.

Companion App

Sound Dashboard & Emergency SOS

A real-time mobile dashboard showing live sound levels, device battery status, and emergency SOS alerts mapped to GPS coordinates — already in development, accessible at login.

Cochlea Cap 4.0

INMP441 Sensors + On-Device ML

Higher-fidelity INMP441 microphones combined with on-device machine learning to classify sound type — distinguishing sirens from car horns, for example — with field testing directly alongside deaf users.

Cochlea Cap 5.0

LiDAR-Based 3D Surroundings Map

LiDAR sensors scanning the environment in real-time and feeding a live 3D perceptual map to the companion app — the same spatial awareness paradigm used in autonomous vehicle perception stacks.
